## Changes from the source

- **Duplicates merged:** Spirit of Eden and At Fillmore East each appeared twice, once per owned pressing. Each is one row now, with both copies listed.
- **Sgt. Pepper's:** the source listed it as "Lonely Hearts Band"; it's corrected to the full title, "Lonely Hearts Club Band".
- **Rumours:** the source title "Rumours = 噂" is the Japanese pressing's title, and "Fleetwood Mac, Fleetwood Mac" was a doubled credit. Both are cleaned up.
- **Yankee Hotel Foxtrot:** the entry was cut off. The artist (Wilco) is filled in from the album and label; genres and pressing year are still missing.
- **Header text dropped:** "Genres / Action / Owned" came from the page itself, not from the albums.
