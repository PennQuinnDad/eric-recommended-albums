A listening list

Eric's Recommended Albums

I'm an album guy. I like starting at track one and letting a record play out the way the band meant it to. Every album here holds up that way, start to finish, deep cuts and all. They run from 1963 to 2026. Some have been with me for decades and some just came out, and they're all ones I keep telling family and friends to put on.

    Every one of these is on my shelf on vinyl.
